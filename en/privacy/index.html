Privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit xtra-ai.com, fill in the free diagnostic form, write to our AI assistant on the website, email us, or message us on Facebook Messenger. It describes why we use the data, who we share it with, how long we keep it and what your rights are.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is Xtra AI.

For any question about your personal data, email us at xtraaibg@gmail.com.

2. The free diagnostic form

When you fill in the form, we receive what you enter: name, email, phone, company website, industry, a convenient day and time for a call, your preferred way to talk, and your answers about your business (team size, tasks, tools, goals).

The form sends this data as an email to our mailbox through the mail server of the website's hosting. The IP address the form was sent from and the time are added to the email automatically. To prevent abuse, the server keeps an encoded (hashed) version of the IP address for 10 minutes.

We use the data to contact you, run the free diagnostic and send you an offer. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and the consent you give in the form (Art. 6(1)(a)).

3. The AI assistant on the website

The website chat is run by an AI assistant (Alex), not a person. The messages you write are sent to our server at Cloudflare and to an AI model that writes the answer. Currently this is a model run by Cloudflare (Workers AI). We may also use models from Anthropic (Claude).

We do not store chat conversations on our servers. The conversation history is kept only in your browser (sessionStorage) until you close the tab.

Please do not share sensitive or personal data in the chat that your question does not need. Answers are generated automatically and may contain mistakes.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering visitors' questions about our services (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

4. Emails

Emails you send to xtraaibg@gmail.com arrive in a Google (Gmail) mailbox. We use an AI assistant that recognises the type of email and drafts answers to questions about our services. For this, the text of the email is sent to our server at Cloudflare and to the AI model described in section 3.

Complaints, questions about payments, contracts or personal data, and anything unclear are handled by a person. Replies sent by the AI assistant are signed as such.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries and taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(f) and (b) GDPR).

5. Facebook Messenger

When you message the Xtra AI Facebook Page, the messages are processed by Meta under Meta's Privacy Policy. Our AI assistant may answer them.

The assistant receives the text of the message and an identifier Facebook assigns to your conversation with our Page. It does not receive your profile, photos or friends list. To understand your follow-up questions, it keeps the latest messages of the conversation for up to 24 hours, and the identifiers of messages already answered for up to 7 days, at Cloudflare.

A member of our team can take over the conversation at any time; the assistant then stops replying in that conversation.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

6. Booking a call

The "Free diagnostic" buttons open a Google Calendar booking page. What you enter there (name, email, chosen time) is processed by Google and appears in our calendar. We use it to hold the call. The legal basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

7. Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the website is used: which pages are visited, from what device and browser, from which approximate location, and for how long. Google Analytics sets cookies (“_ga” and “_ga_…”, lasting up to 2 years). The data is kept in Google Analytics for up to 14 months. Google Analytics is turned on only if you click "Accept" in the cookie banner (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Until then it is not loaded and sets no cookies. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with the "Cookie settings" link at the bottom of every page. If you decline, we delete the Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

The website stores your chosen language and your cookie choice in your browser (localStorage), and the chat stores its history and whether it was opened (sessionStorage, until you close the tab). These entries are needed for the website to work and are not sent to us.

8. Who we share data with

We do not sell personal data. We use providers that process data on our behalf and on our instructions:

  • The website's hosting provider: the website server and the mail server the form uses to send its email.
  • Google (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Analytics, Google Apps Script): email, call booking, analytics and the email AI assistant. Google's policy.
  • Cloudflare: the AI assistant's server, the AI model and the temporary memory for Messenger. Cloudflare's policy.
  • Meta (Facebook Messenger): messages to our Facebook Page.
  • Anthropic: only if we use Claude AI models for the answers. Anthropic's policy.

We disclose data to public authorities only when the law requires us to.

9. Transfers outside the EU

Google, Cloudflare, Meta and Anthropic may also process data in the United States. Such transfers rely on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and/or standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

10. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries (form, email, Messenger): up to 24 months after our last contact. If you become a client: for the duration of the contract and as long as accounting and tax laws require.
  • Website chat: not stored on our servers; in your browser until you close the tab.
  • Messenger assistant memory: 24 hours; identifiers of answered messages: 7 days. The conversation itself stays in our Facebook Page inbox until deleted.
  • Form abuse protection: encoded IP address, 10 minutes.
  • Google Analytics: up to 14 months.

11. AI and automated decisions

The AI assistant only answers questions and drafts replies. It does not make decisions that have legal effects on you or similarly significantly affect you. You can always ask to talk to a person: email us at xtraaibg@gmail.com or book a free diagnostic.

12. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • get information about and a copy of the personal data we process about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data erased ("right to be forgotten");
  • restrict processing;
  • receive your data in a machine-readable format (portability);
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing already carried out;
  • lodge a complaint with the Bulgarian data protection authority, the Commission for Personal Data Protection (2 Prof. Tsvetan Lazarov Blvd., Sofia 1592), or the authority in your EU country.

To exercise your rights, email us at xtraaibg@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

13. How to request deletion of your data

  1. Email xtraaibg@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion".
  2. Tell us where we got your data from: the form, email, the website chat or Facebook Messenger. For Messenger, include your Facebook profile name so we can find the conversation.
  3. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email. Data the law requires us to keep (for example accounting records) is kept only for the required period.

You can also delete the Messenger conversation on your side yourself. The copy kept by Facebook is managed by Meta under its own policy.

14. Security

The website and the connections to our servers use encryption (HTTPS). Access to the data is limited to the people working on your enquiry, and the keys to our systems are stored securely.

15. Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed at people under 16. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

16. Changes to this policy

We may change this policy when we change how we handle data. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page.
